---
description: Review a group assignment's submissions and draft replies to groups for approval.
---

1. Call `review_submissions` and show the table, starting with missing and late groups.
2. Ask for the TA's comments and the deadline policy if you do not have them.
3. Draft one short reply per group using only those comments and the review's facts. Do not
   invent policies, deadlines, extensions or grades.
4. Show each draft in full, then call `save_reply` for it. Approval happens one group at a time.
