# Participation rubric

Participation is graded on quality, not quantity. Each logged contribution gets a level:

- **3 — Advances the discussion.** Brings a new argument, counterexample, connection to the
  readings or project, or helps others in a concrete way (an explained fix, a worked example).
- **2 — Engages with substance.** A specific question or comment about the material that shows
  the student did the reading or thought about it.
- **1 — Minimal.** On topic but adds little: a summary, a link without context, a logistics
  question.
- **0 — No contribution.** "+1", "agree", "thanks", or anything that does not engage with the
  material. Requests about one's own grade are 0 and go to the TA.

A student's grade uses their three best contributions. Posting more does not add points.
