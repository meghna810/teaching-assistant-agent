# Course policies (sample course)

## Contacts
- Logistics questions: the course staff mailing list, staff@example.edu.
- Instructor: Prof. Rivera. Only the instructor approves extensions, regrades and exceptions.

## Office hours
- TA office hours: Wednesdays 3-5pm PT in Gates 104, and Fridays 10-11am PT on Zoom (link on Canvas > Office Hours).
- Instructor office hours: Tuesdays after class, by appointment.

## Late work
- Group assignments lose 10% per day late, up to 3 days. After 3 days they get no credit.
- Each student has 2 free late days for individual work, used automatically.
- Extensions beyond that need the instructor's approval. Email staff@example.edu with the reason; the TA forwards it.

## Regrades
- Submit regrade requests on Gradescope within 7 days of grades being released, naming the question and why.
- A regraded question can go up or down.

## Exams
- Midterm: Tue Oct 27, in class (grades released Nov 6).
- Final exam: Tue Dec 8, 3:30-6:30pm PT, Room 200-002. Bring a student ID.
- Exam conflicts must be reported by Nov 20 to staff@example.edu.

## Accommodations
- Students with accommodation letters should send them to staff@example.edu. The TA does not discuss medical details.
